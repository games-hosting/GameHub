DEAD BY DAWN
online · horror · asymmetric

📖 How to Play

SETUP

One player creates a lobby and shares the 6-letter code. Everyone else joins. When the host hits START GAME, one player is chosen randomly as the monster. Everyone picks a favourite monster (used if chosen) and a perk before the game starts.

☠ THE MONSTER

Goal: Kill all survivors before they escape.

WASD / Arrows — move  ·  E — attack (injures) or hook nearby injured survivor  ·  Q — use your special ability

Hit a survivor once to injure them (they slow down). Hit them again to hook them. A hooked survivor dies after 60s unless rescued.

MONSTERS

👻 Wraith — Q turns invisible for 6s  ·  🪓 Brute — Q lunges forward for a huge range hit  ·  🕯️ Witch — Q teleports to a random generator  ·  🐰 White Rabbit — Q spawns a phantom decoy copy  ·  🎸 Jeffrey — Q freezes all nearby survivors for 3s

🔦 SURVIVORS

Goal: Repair 3 generators, then open an exit gate and escape.

WASD / Arrows — move  ·  Shift — run (faster but noisier)  ·  E — context action (see below)

E — CONTEXT ACTIONS (in priority order)

Near a hooked survivor → hold E to unhook them (3.5s)

Near a ⚙ generator → hold E to repair it (fills over ~70s)

Near a EXIT gate (after 3 gens done) → hold E to open it (10s)

Near a locker → press E to hide inside (cancels heartbeat)

Repair 3 of 5 generators to power the exit gates. Then open a gate and walk through to escape. Half the survivors need to escape to win.

If you're hooked, wiggle (spam any key) for a small chance to escape. Or wait for a teammate!

⚡ PERKS

MONSTER PERKS

🩸 Bloodhound — See red scratch marks left by running survivors  ·  💀 Devour Hope — Get faster with each hook  ·  ⚡ NOED — After all gens are done, your hits are one-shot hooks  ·  🔧 Ruin — Unattended generators slowly regress

SURVIVOR PERKS

💨 Sprint Burst — First run burst gives 3s of extra speed  ·  🌿 Botany — Repair gens 25% faster  ·  🤫 Iron Will — (visual indicator only)  ·  ⚔ Decisive Strike — Stun the monster once after being unhooked

The heartbeat on your screen means the monster is close. Hide in a locker to suppress it!
